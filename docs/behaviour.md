# How the assistant behaves

| A customer writes | What they get |
|---|---|
| A question the business's pages answer | A short answer that names the page it came from |
| A question the business's pages don't cover | The callback reply |
| An urgent message | The fixed urgent reply, and nothing else. The business's pages are not read |
| A price | The amount as the business's pages write it, or the callback reply when that amount isn't there |

The Hours & FAQ page answers these three. An after-hours question is not an urgent message, because it uses none of the urgent words.

| A customer writes | What they get |
|---|---|
| Do you install septic systems? | No, we don't install or service septic systems. From Hours & FAQ. |
| Are you licensed and insured? | Yes, we're fully licensed and insured in New Jersey. From Hours & FAQ. |
| Do you offer after-hours emergency callouts? | Yes, we handle emergencies after hours too. Just call our main line at (856) 555-0142. From Hours & FAQ. |
