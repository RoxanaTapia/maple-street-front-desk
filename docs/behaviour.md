# How your assistant behaves

This page is for you, Dave. Open it when you want to see what a customer experiences: the kind of message they send, and what they get back. The exact words of the urgent reply and the callback live in [What we've agreed](agreements.md). The path behind a reply is in [How the assistant works](how-it-works.md).

| A customer writes | What they get |
|---|---|
| A question your pages answer | A short answer that names the page it came from |
| A question your pages don't cover | The callback reply |
| An urgent message | The fixed urgent reply, and nothing else. Your pages are not read |
| A price | The amount as your pages write it, or the callback reply when that amount isn't there |
